The rule implementation, regular-ending compatibility choices in policy.tsv,
and finite irregular spelling choices in irregulars.tsv are offered under MIT.
The rules preserve the existing Kmeow typing contract. The MIT grant covers
Kmeow's implementation and original compilation/contributions to the extent
the copyright holder holds those rights. It does not claim exclusive ownership
or independent discovery of ordinary English spellings or grammatical facts,
and does not grant rights owned by other parties.

The former imported expanded-form catalog is not part of this implementation.
Historical reference downloads, notices and verification artifacts remain in
private development backups, outside the runtime and standalone source export.
No source dictionary, reference corpus or pronunciation data is required to
build or use these rules.
